About BMN

A newsroom built around evidence, access and community.

BarbWire Media Network produces original reporting, interviews, public-affairs coverage, multimedia journalism, commentary and community-centered storytelling. BMN distinguishes reported journalism from opinion, commentary, satire, entertainment and sponsored material through clear labeling.

Editorial Standards

Receipts over rumors.

BMN is committed to accuracy, transparency, attribution and responsible newsgathering.

  • Primary documents whenever reasonably available
  • Sources and disputed claims clearly attributed
  • News separated from opinion and commentary
  • Material factual errors corrected transparently
  • Conflicts disclosed when relevant to coverage
  • AI-assisted presentation does not replace verification
