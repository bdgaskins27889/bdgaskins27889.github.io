# Follow the Funding: reporting plan (not a published investigation)

## Editorial scope
Community violence intervention, violence prevention, street outreach and public safety are related but distinct areas. Avoid conflating activities, credentials, grant purposes or outcome metrics.

## First reporting package
1. Identify one specific funding program, geography, agency, grant cycle and award recipient using official records.
2. Obtain award notices, executed contracts, amendments, scopes of work, subaward records and payment information where public.
3. Match contractual deliverables to contemporaneous reports and independent evaluations. Do not infer nonperformance from missing public data alone.
4. Check evaluation methodology, baseline, timeframe, denominators and whether outcomes were independently validated.
5. Seek meaningful comment from the funder, funded organization and people directly affected, while protecting privacy and safety.
6. Disclose relevant relationships or interests of BMN personnel and keep reporting decisions separate from advocacy and consulting work.
7. Complete legal/ethics review, evidence log, headline check and publisher approval before publishing.

## Evidence log columns
Claim ID | Exact claim | Source title | Issuer | Date | Primary URL | Page/section | Independent corroboration | Status (verified/disputed/unverified) | Response sought | Reviewer | Publication decision

## Public records request starter
Subject: Public records request: [program name], [fiscal years]

Please provide existing records concerning [program name and jurisdiction] for [date range], including final grant award notices, executed agreements, scopes of work, contract amendments, payment or reimbursement records, required performance reports, monitoring reports and final evaluations, to the extent public under applicable law. Electronic copies are preferred. If any portion is withheld, please identify the applicable legal basis and release reasonably segregable portions. Please advise of any fees before processing if they would exceed [amount].

This is a template only. Confirm the applicable jurisdiction, custodian and records law before sending. Do not send automatically.

## Publication gate
No named organization, award amount, failure allegation or numerical outcome may be published without primary source verification and editorial approval. Do not put confidential source records in this public repository.
