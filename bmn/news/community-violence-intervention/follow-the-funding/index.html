Follow the Funding

Where does community violence intervention and public safety funding go, what is delivered, and how are results independently assessed?

Questions guiding this reporting

Funding

Who receives the money?

Public awards, subawards, contracts, amendments, payment records and disclosed relationships.

Delivery

What services were provided?

Contract deliverables, staffing, training, outreach, intervention and prevention activities.

Results

What can be measured?

Definitions, baselines, evaluation designs, independent findings and limitations.

Oversight

Who is accountable?

Monitoring, audit findings, complaints, corrective actions and agency responses.

Publication status

Project announced, no findings published. This page describes questions under examination. It does not allege wrongdoing by any person, agency or organization.

Individual investigations will be published here only after documentary verification, fair opportunity to respond, legal and ethical review, and publisher approval.

How we report

We prioritize original public records and attributable evidence, distinguish allegations from findings, document relevant conflicts of interest, and publish corrections when warranted. See BMN editorial standards.
