Editorial Standards, AI Authorship & Corrections

BarbWire Media Network (BMN) is an independent digital newsroom covering public affairs, criminal justice, reentry, community violence intervention, culture, and accountability.

Verification and attribution

We seek primary documents and firsthand accounts where practical. Allegations are attributed, uncertainty is identified, and a person's denial or response is included when material and available. A document's existence does not, by itself, establish that every claim within it is true.

News, opinion, and satire

Reporting, analysis, opinion, satire, sponsored content, and promotional material should be distinctly labeled. Headlines and graphics should not overstate evidence.

Corrections

Material errors should be corrected promptly with a dated correction note describing the change. Substantive updates should be distinguished from corrections. Requests for correction may be sent through BMN's published social-media contact channels at the editorial contact listed on our press page.

Independence and conflicts

Relevant affiliations, advocacy work, financial interests, or direct involvement in matters covered should be disclosed when they could affect readers' assessment of a story. Editorial judgments should not be sold to sources or sponsors.

AI tools, authorship, and editorial responsibility

Human editors determine BMN's reporting priorities, editorial positions, sourcing standards, and publication decisions. AI tools may assist with finding research leads, organizing notes, summarizing source material, drafting, editing, transcription, and multimedia production, subject to human oversight. AI output is never treated as proof of a fact: staff review claims against original, attributable evidence and check significant quotations, dates, names, statistics, and legal assertions before publication.

We distinguish human-originated ideas from AI-composed wording. Expertise, original reporting, firsthand experience, arguments, and editorial direction may come from the named journalist, while AI may still have produced some or all of a draft's language. We will not claim that substantially AI-drafted wording is exclusively human-written.

Transparency: BMN will identify material generative-AI assistance in published reporting or opinion when it meaningfully shapes the final text, image, audio, or video. A disclosure may read: “Editorial note: AI assisted with research organization and drafting. The publisher reviewed the article and verified material claims.” Routine spelling and punctuation assistance ordinarily does not require such a note. An AI-created illustration must be clearly labeled and cannot be passed off as authentic documentary evidence.

Source integrity and safety: We do not invent interviews, quotations, eyewitness accounts, records, or citations; we do not use AI-generated imagery as evidence; and we protect sensitive source identities and private records from inappropriate disclosure to external tools. Any substantial alteration of genuine visual or audio evidence must be clearly explained. Human editors are accountable for corrections.

Outside submissions and publication requirements

Before offering a guest opinion, reported story, image, or other work to an outside publisher, BMN checks that publication's current rules governing AI assistance, authorship, exclusivity, simultaneous submissions, original publication, attribution, and permissions. When a publisher prohibits AI-generated wording, BMN will not submit AI-drafted or substantially AI-rewritten text as a human-authored article even when its central ideas are human-originated. Authors must comply with the publisher's permitted writing process. If an exclusive submission is pending, the same manuscript will not be sent elsewhere until released from exclusivity or withdrawn.

Media pitches and factual press releases are distinct from exclusive guest opinion manuscripts, but each outlet's rules and embargoes still apply. BMN maintains an outreach log of submissions, deadlines, responses, corrections, and withdrawal requests to avoid duplication.

Privacy and safety

We weigh public interest against risks to children, survivors, confidential sources, and private individuals. We avoid publishing unnecessary identifying details.

Updated November 20, 2026. This policy describes BMN's practices; outside outlets may impose stricter conditions.
